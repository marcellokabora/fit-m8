# **Sports & Activity Catalog: Barcelona**

Barcelona's unique geography—bounded by the Mediterranean coast on the east and the Collserola natural park on the west—makes it a premier hub for diverse athletic disciplines. This document provides a structured taxonomy of sports available in the city, breaking down major activities into sub-disciplines (such as surfboard variations, court surfaces, and beach sports) alongside their mapping to FIT-M8 database ActivityIds.

## **Hierarchical Activity Taxonomy**

### **1\. Board & Water Sports (Aqua)**

Water activities are grouped by craft, style, and hydro-board type.

* **Surfing & Surfboard Disciplines** (surf)  
  * *Shortboard Surfing* (Performance & wave maneuvers)  
  * *Longboard / Malibu Surfing* (Traditional style & nose-riding)  
  * *Fish / Funboard Surfing* (Small to medium wave cruisers)  
  * *Foil Surfing & Hydrofoiling* (Ocean & swell foiling)  
  * *Windsurfing & Kitesurfing* (Wind-assisted board sports along the coastline)  
* **Stand-up Paddleboarding (SUP)** (paddleBoard)  
  * *Flatwater SUP Touring* (Port Vell & Barceloneta coastline)  
  * *SUP Yoga & Pilates* (On-water stability training)  
  * *SUP Wave Riding*  
* **Paddled & Towed Watercraft**  
  * *Kayak* (Sea kayaking along Bogatell & Mar Bella)  
  * *Canoe & Outrigger*  
  * *Wakeboarding & Cable Park* (Towed water skiing & board riding)  
* **Swimming & Aquatic Fitness** (swimming)  
  * *Open Water Sea Swimming* (Coastal routes & sea channels)  
  * *Lap Swimming* (Indoor & outdoor municipal pools)

### **2\. Racquet & Paddle Sports**

Encompasses traditional racquet sports, court variations, and beach-specific paddle games.

* **Tennis** (tennis)  
  * *Clay Court Tennis* (Dominant in local Barcelona clubs)  
  * *Hard Court / Synthetic Tennis*  
  * *Beach Tennis* (Paddle/racquet sport played on sand courts)  
* **Padel & Pickleball**  
  * *Padel* (padel) — Wall-enclosed doubles play (highly popular across Catalonia)  
  * *Pickleball* (pickleBall) — Low-impact court paddle sport  
* **Court Racquets**  
  * *Badminton* (badminton)  
  * *Squash & Racquetball* (squash)  
  * *Table Tennis / Ping-Pong* (pingPong)  
* **Beach Racquet**  
  * *Frescobol / Mat surface wooden paddle play* (frescobol)

### **3\. Beach & Coastal Activities**

Sand and coastal disciplines optimized for Barceloneta, Nova Icària, and Mar Bella beaches.

* **Sand Court Sports**  
  * *Beach Volleyball* (beachVolley) — 2v2 and 4v4 sand play  
  * *Footvolley* (footVolley) — Sand volleyball played without hands  
  * *Beach Tennis* (Standard sand court paddle tennis)  
  * *Frescobol* (frescobol) — Casual non-competitive shore racquet rallies  
* **Coastal Outdoor Conditioning**  
  * *Beach Bootcamp & Sand Circuit Training* (bootCamp)  
  * *Sunrise / Sunset Shoreline Yoga*

### **4\. Outdoor, Urban & Adventure Sports**

Activities taking advantage of urban plazas, coastal boardwalks, and surrounding mountain ranges.

* **Wheel Mobility & Skate**  
  * *Skateboarding & Bowl Riding* (skateBoard) — Street & park skateboarding (MACBA, Forum)  
  * *Surfskate & Carver* (surfSkate) — Coastal promenade carving & wave-simulation skateboarding  
  * *Rollerblading & Inline Skating* (rollerBlade)  
  * *BMX & Freestyle Biking* (bmx) — Street, park, and bowl freestyle riding (Forum, Mar Bella skateparks)  
  * *Stunt & Freestyle Scootering* (scootering) — Urban street & skatepark freestyle riding (MACBA, Forum, Mar Bella)  
* **Trail & Mountain**  
  * *Running & Trail Running* (jogging) — Urban boardwalk runs & Collserola hill trails  
  * *Hiking & Trekking* (hiking) — Collserola, Montjuïc, and day trips (Montserrat)  
  * *Golf* (golf) — Pitch & putt and championship courses  
* **Climbing**  
  * *Bouldering* (rockClimbing) — Indoor gym climbing  
  * *Sport / Outdoor Rock Climbing* (rockClimbing) — Natural crags around Barcelona province

### **5\. Combat & Martial Arts (martialArts)**

Categorized by combat style and discipline.

* **Striking Sports**  
  * *Boxing*  
  * *Kickboxing & K-1*  
  * *Muay Thai (Thai Boxing)*  
  * *Karate*  
* **Grappling & Ground Fighting**  
  * *Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)* (Gi & No-Gi)  
  * *Judo*  
* **Hybrid & Self-Defense**  
  * *Krav Maga*  
  * *Mixed Martial Arts (MMA)*

### **6\. Cycling & Mobility (cycling)**

* *Outdoor Road Cycling* (Routes to Tibidabo, Montseny, coastal roads)  
* *Gravel & Mountain Biking (MTB)* (Collserola trails)  
* *Indoor Cycling / Spinning* (Rhythmic studio cycling)  
* *Mobility & Functional Flexibility Workouts*

### **7\. Fitness, Strength & Calisthenics**

* *Gym Open Training* (gym) — Standard weightlifting & cardio equipment  
* *Calisthenics & Bodyweight* (calisthenics) — Outdoor parks (Barceloneta beachfront park, Ciutadella)  
* *Functional Fitness & Cross-training* (crossTraining) — High-intensity functional movement circuits & HIIT  
* *Bootcamp* (bootCamp) — Group outdoor beach/park conditioning circuits  
* *Bodybuilding* — Hypertrophy-focused resistance training

### **8\. Mind, Body & Recovery**

* **Yoga** (yoga)  
  * *Vinyasa & Power Flow*  
  * *Hatha & Alignment Yoga*  
  * *Yin & Restorative Yoga*  
  * *Hot Yoga / Bikram*  
* **Pilates** (pilates)  
  * *Mat Pilates*  
  * *Reformer Pilates*  
* **Mental & Respiratory Health**  
  * *Meditation*  
  * *Breathwork & Pranayama*  
* **Wellness & Recovery** (wellnessRecovery)  
  * *Sauna & Steam Baths*  
  * *Cryotherapy*  
  * *Sports Massage & Therapy*  
  * *Hydrotherapy & Spa Facilities*  
  * *Infrared Cabins*

### **9\. Dance, Music & Performing Arts**

* **Social & Partner Dance** (socialDance)  
  * *Salsa* (Cuban & Line style)  
  * *Bachata* (Sensual & Traditional)  
  * *Kizomba & Zouk*  
* **Solo & Studio Dance** (socialDance)  
  * *Hip Hop & Street Dance*

## **Recommendations for FIT-M8 Activity Hierarchy**

1. **Sub-Type System**: Introduce a subType or discipline field to ActivityId so that surf can store metadata (shortboard, longboard, sup, foil) or pilates can store (mat, reformer) without cluttering the top-level activity list.  
2. **Beach & Environment Tag**: Add a locationType: \['beach', 'indoor', 'outdoor\_park', 'court'\] flag to support activities that span multiple environments (e.g., Beach Volleyball vs. Indoor Volleyball, or Beach Yoga vs. Studio Yoga).  
3. **Complete Mapping Coverage**: With scootering, bmx, socialDance, aerialFitness, and wellnessRecovery integrated, virtually all urban and coastal activities in Barcelona have assigned camelCase ActivityId mappings.
