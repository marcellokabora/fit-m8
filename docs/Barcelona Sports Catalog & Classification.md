# **Sports & Activity Catalog: Barcelona**

Barcelona's unique geography—bounded by the Mediterranean coast on the east and the Collserola natural park on the west—makes it a premier hub for diverse athletic disciplines. This document provides a structured taxonomy of sports available in the city, breaking down major activities into sub-disciplines (such as surfboard variations, court surfaces, and beach sports) alongside their mapping to FIT-M8 database ActivityIds.

## **Hierarchical Activity Taxonomy**

### **1\. Board & Water Sports (Aqua)**

Water activities are grouped by craft, style, and hydro-board type.

- **Surfing & Surfboard Disciplines** (surf)
  - _Shortboard Surfing_ (Performance & wave maneuvers)
  - _Longboard / Malibu Surfing_ (Traditional style & nose-riding)
  - _Fish / Funboard Surfing_ (Small to medium wave cruisers)
  - _Foil Surfing & Hydrofoiling_ (Ocean & swell foiling)
  - _Windsurfing & Kitesurfing_ (Wind-assisted board sports along the coastline)
- **Stand-up Paddleboarding (SUP)** (paddleBoard)
  - _Flatwater SUP Touring_ (Port Vell & Barceloneta coastline)
  - _SUP Yoga & Pilates_ (On-water stability training)
  - _SUP Wave Riding_
- **Paddled & Towed Watercraft**
  - _Kayak_ (Sea kayaking along Bogatell & Mar Bella)
  - _Canoe & Outrigger_
  - _Wakeboarding & Cable Park_ (Towed water skiing & board riding)
- **Swimming & Aquatic Fitness** (swimming)
  - _Open Water Sea Swimming_ (Coastal routes & sea channels)
  - _Lap Swimming_ (Indoor & outdoor municipal pools)

### **2\. Racquet & Paddle Sports**

Encompasses traditional racquet sports, court variations, and beach-specific paddle games.

- **Tennis** (tennis)
  - _Clay Court Tennis_ (Dominant in local Barcelona clubs)
  - _Hard Court / Synthetic Tennis_
  - _Beach Tennis_ (Paddle/racquet sport played on sand courts)
- **Padel & Pickleball**
  - _Padel_ (padel) — Wall-enclosed doubles play (highly popular across Catalonia)
  - _Pickleball_ (pickleBall) — Low-impact court paddle sport
- **Court Racquets**
  - _Badminton_ (badminton)
  - _Squash & Racquetball_ (squash)
  - _Table Tennis / Ping-Pong_ (pingPong)
- **Beach Racquet**
  - _Frescobol / Mat surface wooden paddle play_ (frescobol)

### **3\. Beach & Coastal Activities**

Sand and coastal disciplines optimized for Barceloneta, Nova Icària, and Mar Bella beaches.

- **Sand Court Sports**
  - _Beach Volleyball_ (beachVolley) — 2v2 and 4v4 sand play
  - _Footvolley_ (footVolley) — Sand volleyball played without hands
  - _Beach Tennis_ (Standard sand court paddle tennis)
  - _Frescobol_ (frescobol) — Casual non-competitive shore racquet rallies
- **Coastal Outdoor Conditioning**
  - _Beach Bootcamp & Sand Circuit Training_ (bootCamp)
  - _Sunrise / Sunset Shoreline Yoga_

### **4\. Outdoor, Urban & Adventure Sports**

Activities taking advantage of urban plazas, coastal boardwalks, and surrounding mountain ranges.

- **Wheel Mobility & Skate**
  - _Skateboarding & Bowl Riding_ (skateBoard) — Street & park skateboarding (MACBA, Forum)
  - _Surfskate & Carver_ (surfSkate) — Coastal promenade carving & wave-simulation skateboarding
  - _Rollerblading & Inline Skating_ (rollerBlade)
  - _BMX & Freestyle Biking_ (bmx) — Street, park, and bowl freestyle riding (Forum, Mar Bella skateparks)
  - _Stunt & Freestyle Scootering_ (scootering) — Urban street & skatepark freestyle riding (MACBA, Forum, Mar Bella)
- **Trail & Mountain**
  - _Running & Trail Running_ (jogging) — Urban boardwalk runs & Collserola hill trails
  - _Hiking & Trekking_ (hiking) — Collserola, Montjuïc, and day trips (Montserrat)
  - _Golf_ (golf) — Pitch & putt and championship courses
- **Climbing**
  - _Bouldering_ (rockClimbing) — Indoor gym climbing
  - _Sport / Outdoor Rock Climbing_ (rockClimbing) — Natural crags around Barcelona province

### **5\. Combat & Martial Arts (martialArts)**

Categorized by combat style and discipline.

- **Striking Sports**

  - _Kickboxing & K-1_
  - _Muay Thai (Thai Boxing)_

- **Grappling & Ground Fighting**

  - _Judo_
- **Hybrid & Self-Defense**
  - _Krav Maga_
  - _Mixed Martial Arts (MMA)_

### **6\. Cycling & Mobility (cycling)**

- _Outdoor Road Cycling_ (Routes to Tibidabo, Montseny, coastal roads)
- _Gravel & Mountain Biking (MTB)_ (Collserola trails)
- _Indoor Cycling / Spinning_ (Rhythmic studio cycling)
- _Mobility & Functional Flexibility Workouts_

### **7\. Fitness, Strength & Calisthenics**

- _Gym Open Training_ (gym) — Standard weightlifting & cardio equipment
- _Calisthenics & Bodyweight_ (calisthenics) — Outdoor parks (Barceloneta beachfront park, Ciutadella)

- _Cross-training_ (crossTraining) — High-intensity functional movement circuits & HIIT
- _Functional Fitness_ (functionalFitness) — Multi-joint, movement-based conditioning
- _Bootcamp_ (bootCamp) — Group outdoor beach/park conditioning circuits

- **Wellness & Recovery** (wellnessRecovery)

  - _Cryotherapy_

  - _Hydrotherapy & Spa Facilities_
  - _Infrared Cabins_

### **9\. Dance, Music & Performing Arts**

- **Social & Partner Dance** (socialDance)
  - _Salsa_ (Cuban & Line style)
  - _Bachata_ (Sensual & Traditional)
  - _Kizomba & Zouk_
- **Solo & Studio Dance** (socialDance)
  - _Hip Hop & Street Dance_

## **Recommendations for FIT-M8 Activity Hierarchy**

1. **Sub-Type System**: Introduce a subType or discipline field to ActivityId so that surf can store metadata (shortboard, longboard, sup, foil) or pilates can store (mat, reformer) without cluttering the top-level activity list.
2. **Beach & Environment Tag**: Add a locationType: \['beach', 'indoor', 'outdoor_park', 'court'\] flag to support activities that span multiple environments (e.g., Beach Volleyball vs. Indoor Volleyball, or Beach Yoga vs. Studio Yoga).
3. **Complete Mapping Coverage**: With scootering, bmx, socialDance, aerialFitness, and wellnessRecovery integrated, virtually all urban and coastal activities in Barcelona have assigned camelCase ActivityId mappings.
